# SIGRID – Product Concept

## Vision
Sigrid is a digital sewing platform that makes garment-making accessible to complete beginners. By combining AI-generated custom patterns with animated 3D step-by-step guidance, Sigrid removes the two biggest barriers to sewing: fear of failure and not knowing what to do next.

## Primary User: Emma
Emma is 24. She wants to make her own clothes but has never picked up a needle and thread. She's afraid of making mistakes and wasting money on fabric. She needs constant reassurance, clear instructions, and a sense of progress. Every screen must pass the Emma test: would she understand this immediately and feel confident?

## Core Flow
1. **Onboarding** – Meet Sigrid, enter measurements, create account
2. **Discover** – Browse garments and creator patterns
3. **Customise** – Choose fit, confirm measurements
4. **Pattern** – Receive a custom-generated pattern fitted to Emma's body
5. **Materials** – Know exactly what to buy and where to get it
6. **Make** – Follow animated 3D step-by-step guide with a real camera that zooms to the right spot
7. **Celebrate** – Share the finished garment, build a wardrobe

## Design Philosophy
- **Calm confidence** – Never overwhelming. One thing at a time.
- **Progress always visible** – Emma knows exactly where she is and how far she has to go.
- **iOS-like clarity** – Clean, warm, minimal. Animations are purposeful, not decorative.
- **Body positivity** – Patterns are made for Emma's exact measurements. No standard sizes.

## The 3D Sewing Guide
This is Sigrid's killer feature. Instead of a flat PDF with confusing diagrams, Emma sees a 3D garment on a mannequin. As she moves through each step, the camera floats to the relevant part of the garment, which glows softly to draw her eye. She never has to wonder where to look or what to do next.

## Technical Stack
- Next.js 16 App Router + TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS v4 for styling
- Framer Motion for all animations
- Three.js for the 3D sewing guide
- CSS variables for the colour system (easily swappable)
